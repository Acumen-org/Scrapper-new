# Bellwether

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web, used on desktop and mobile.

## Users
Acumen Strategy administrators, product owners, and normal users researching advisory firms for PHH, Glynac and AcuBooth.

## Product Purpose
Find suitable firms, understand the evidence behind product fit, and reach the people working there. Preserve the underlying intelligence while reducing how much must be read at once.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Firm, people, activity, scoring, saved-list and enrichment workflows. Scoring must distinguish missing evidence from known facts. Product owners edit their assigned product; administrators manage settings. Microsoft sign-in is the preferred authentication method, with the designated Acumen administrator provisioned on sign-in.

Contact discovery runs in the background. Unverified inferred email addresses must not appear as usable contacts. Contact absence must remain truthful; no fabricated addresses or phone numbers. Verification does not guarantee delivery.

## Brand Commitments
The user requires neutral dark grey, restrained red accents, a new logo, professional restrained login, a centered conversational AI experience, breathing room, and a substantial replacement of the previous dense table and dossier presentation. No blue surfaces or theatrical login graphics.

## Operating Context
The user explicitly confirmed that all workflows matter: this is a GTM intelligence app for every available, true fact. Firm discovery, in-depth research, people/contact intelligence and ongoing monitoring must have equally clear routes; none is relegated to an obscure secondary mode.
